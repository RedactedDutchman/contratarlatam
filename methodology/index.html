Methodology & disclosure

Affiliate disclosure

ContratarLATAM may earn a commission when you sign up with a provider through some links on this site. This does not change the prices you pay, and it does not affect which providers we list or how we order them. Providers can't pay to change their published figures here.

Not legal or tax advice

Everything on this site is general information. Labour and tax rules change. Get advice from a qualified professional in the relevant country before hiring or terminating anyone.

How we collect figures

  • Every number links to the page it came from and carries the date we checked it.
  • Provider prices are the "starting from" figures published on each provider's own pricing page. If a provider doesn't publish a price, we say "Not published".
  • Statutory rates come from official sources (ARCA, InfoLEG, Boletín Oficial, gov.co portals) or reputable law firms and advisers when no clear official summary exists.
  • If we can't find reliable rates for a country, we leave it out of the estimator rather than guess.

How the estimator works

It multiplies the gross monthly salary by the cited rates and adds them up, then shows the total per month and per year. It is an estimate: it leaves out EOR fees, optional benefits, collective-agreement items, caps and incentive schemes unless stated. The rate file is public: rates.json.

Corrections

If you spot an outdated figure, please let us know through the newsletter page once it opens; corrections are welcome.

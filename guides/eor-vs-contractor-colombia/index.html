EOR vs contractor in Colombia

Checked 2026-10-28. Not legal or tax advice.

As an employee through an EOR

  • The employer pays pension (12%), ARL, the 4% family fund and, above 10 minimum wages or for non-exempt employers, health, SENA and ICBF (VUE, ET 114-1).
  • The worker receives prima, cesantías with interest and 15 working days of vacation (Holland & Knight).
  • EOR fee on top, e.g. From $599 per employee/month (annual contracts) at Remote, From $499 per employee/month at Papaya Global (all providers).

As a contractor

  • No prima, cesantías or paid vacation from the client.
  • Contractor platforms list lower fees, e.g. Remote from $29, Skuad payments from $19 and Deel from $49 per contractor per month (Remote, Skuad, Deel).

Misclassification risk

Colombia's labour code presumes that any personal service relationship is governed by an employment contract (Código Sustantivo del Trabajo art. 24, CST). Contractor-of-record services (Deel and Remote from $325, Papaya Global from $199 a month) hold the contract instead.

EOR vs contractor in Mexico

Checked 2026-10-28. Not legal or tax advice.

As an employee through an EOR

  • The employer pays IMSS quotas, 2% retirement, the 2026 old-age rate (3.150%–7.513%), 5% INFONAVIT and state payroll tax (CMS Consultores).
  • The worker receives at least 15 days' aguinaldo, 12 vacation days in year one with a 25% premium, and profit sharing (Gloroots).
  • EOR fee on top, e.g. From $599 per employee/month (annual contracts) at Remote, From $499 per employee/month at Papaya Global (all providers).

As a contractor

  • No aguinaldo, vacation premium, IMSS or INFONAVIT from the client.
  • Contractor platforms list lower fees, e.g. Remote from $29, Skuad payments from $19 and Deel from $49 per contractor per month (Remote, Skuad, Deel).

Misclassification risk

Mexico's Federal Labour Law defines an employment relationship as subordinated personal work for pay and presumes it between whoever provides personal work and whoever receives it (LFT arts. 20–21, LFT). Contractor-of-record services (Deel and Remote from $325, Papaya Global from $199 a month) hold the contract instead.

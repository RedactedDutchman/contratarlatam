EOR vs contractor in Brazil

Checked 2026-10-28. Not legal or tax advice.

As an employee through an EOR

  • The employer pays 20% INSS, RAT, third-party contributions and 8% FGTS (Receita Federal, Lei 8.036).
  • The worker receives a 13th salary and vacation with a one-third bonus (Lei 4.090, Constitution).
  • EOR fee on top, e.g. From $599 per employee/month (annual contracts) at Remote, From $499 per employee/month at Papaya Global (all providers).

As a contractor

  • No 13th salary, FGTS or vacation bonus from the client.
  • Contractor platforms list lower fees, e.g. Remote from $29, Skuad payments from $19 and Deel from $49 per contractor per month (Remote, Skuad, Deel).

Misclassification risk

Under the CLT, an employee is a person who provides non-occasional services to an employer under its direction and for a salary (CLT art. 3, CLT); a contractor working that way can be reclassified. Contractor-of-record services (Deel and Remote from $325, Papaya Global from $199 a month) hold the contract instead.

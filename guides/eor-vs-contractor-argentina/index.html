EOR vs contractor in Argentina

Checked 2026-10-28. Not legal or tax advice.

As an employee through an EOR

  • The EOR is the legal employer and pays employer contributions of 20.40% or 18% (ARCA) plus the 6% health fund (Ley 23.660).
  • The worker gets aguinaldo, 14–35 days' paid vacation and the severance protection of LCT art. 245 (LCT).
  • Platform fee on top: e.g. From $599 per EOR employee/month at Deel, From $599 per employee/month (annual contracts) at Remote (all providers).

As a contractor

  • Argentine freelancers usually invoice under the simplified regime (monotributo) or the general regime, administered by ARCA (ARCA monotributo).
  • No aguinaldo, statutory vacation or severance from the client.
  • Contractor platforms charge less: e.g. Remote from $29 and Deel from $49 per contractor per month (Remote, Deel).

The misclassification risk

Under LCT art. 23, providing services in a situation of dependence (subordination) creates a presumption of an employment contract unless the circumstances show otherwise (LCT). If a contractor works like an employee, the company can face employee-level obligations. Contractor-of-record services (from $325 a month at Deel and Remote) put the contract with the provider instead.

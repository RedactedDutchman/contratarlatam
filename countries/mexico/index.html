Employer cost of hiring in Mexico (2026)

Checked 2026-10-28. Not legal or tax advice.

The estimator covers Mexico with stated assumptions: the IMSS contribution base (SBC) is built from the monthly salary plus first-year minimum aguinaldo and vacation premium, capped at 25 UMA (UMA 117.31 a day from February 2026); the old-age rate is picked from the 2026 band table; the work-risk premium and state payroll tax are inputs because they differ per company and state. Real payroll uses the integrated salary including other benefits, so treat the result as an estimate.

State payroll tax (ISN)

Paid by the employer, rate set by each state; around 3% in Mexico City (Glints).

Aguinaldo, vacation and vacation premium

At least 15 days' salary as aguinaldo, paid before 20 December (LFT art. 87). At least 12 vacation days after the first year, rising by 2 days a year to 20 days in year five, then 2 more days for every five years. A vacation premium of at least 25% on vacation pay (LFT art. 80). Sources: Gloroots, Ley Federal del Trabajo (official PDF).

Profit sharing (PTU)

Employers distribute 10% of taxable profit to employees, capped per employee (Gloroots).

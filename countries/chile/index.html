How the estimator handles Chile

It optionally adds the monthly gratificación (capped using the CLP 553,553 minimum taxable income Previred lists for August 2026; the legal cap uses the minimum income in force on 31 December), then applies the 3.5% employer pension contribution and the work-accident rate to pay capped at 90 UF, and unemployment insurance to pay capped at 135.2 UF. Applying the 90 UF cap to the work-accident rate is our assumption. Health (7%) and the AFP 10% are paid by the employee, so they are not included.

Fondo de Asistencia Laboral (FAL)

Ley 27.802 (Labour Modernisation Law, 2026) creates a monthly FAL contribution of 1% for large companies and 2.5% for MiPyMEs (art. 60). Decreto 408/2026 provides that the FAL rate is deducted from the existing employer contribution (art. 24), and ARCA's rate table shows the total staying at 20.40% / 18% (RG 5907 annex). Its start date has been postponed more than once, most recently by Decreto 1228/2026 (source).

Aguinaldo (13th-month pay, SAC)

Paid in two instalments due 30 June and 18 December, each equal to 50% of the highest monthly pay earned in that semester (LCT arts. 121–122, InfoLEG). In practice this adds about one month's salary per year, and contributions apply to it.

Vacation

14 calendar days up to 5 years' service; 21 days for 5–10 years; 28 days for 10–20 years; 35 days over 20 years (LCT art. 150, InfoLEG).

Severance

For dismissal without just cause after the probation period: one month's salary per year of service or fraction over three months, based on the best normal and habitual monthly pay of the last year, subject to a cap tied to the applicable collective agreement and a floor of one month (LCT art. 245, InfoLEG). Notice-period rules and other additional payments also apply. Ley 27.802 amended parts of the labour regime, so check the current text with an adviser.

Estimate the cost

The estimator adds the contribution rate, the 6% health fund and aguinaldo to gross pay. It does not model contribution caps, deductions under Ley 27.430, incentive regimes such as RIFL, collective-agreement items, or ART unless you enter your quote.

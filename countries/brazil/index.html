How the estimator handles Brazil

It adds the 13th salary and the one-third vacation bonus as monthly accruals, then applies INSS, RAT, third-party contributions and FGTS to salary plus those accruals. You choose the RAT rate (default 2%, medium risk) and the third-party rate (default 5.8%). FAP, Simples Nacional, payroll-tax relief schemes, benefits such as meal vouchers, and the dismissal fine are not included.
